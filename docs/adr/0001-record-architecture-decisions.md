# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-24

In the context of a long-lived personal application developed with AI assistance, facing the need
to preserve architectural rationale without duplicating executable specifications or
implementation details, we decided for short Architecture Decision Records under `docs/adr/` and
against living design documents or undocumented decisions, to give future contributors and agents
a durable explanation of broad choices, accepting the maintenance cost of recording and
superseding decisions explicitly.
